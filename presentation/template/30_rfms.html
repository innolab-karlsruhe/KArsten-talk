Es sind wirklich die großen Fortschritte in der Physical Intelligence, die uns hier als Softwareunternehmen haben aufhorchen lassen. Wir haben nämlich jetzt die sogenannten Robot Foundation Models. So, what are Robot Foundation Models (RFMs)?
Before RFMs, the SOTA was to have complex Computer Vision pipelines or specialized models for controlling robots.
So assume, you want your robot to pick up glassware and put it in a container, you would typically engineer a model around that specific use case and that specific robot.
That is of course rather inflexible and does not scale well.

So you might know foundation models from Computer Vision or basically all the LLMs: You have one big model that is pre-trained on a large dataset and has a wide range of general capabilities and world knowledge. From that it can then be adapted for specific tasks. Traditionally through transfer learning or fine-tuning, where you can profit from the pre-trained capabilities and knowledge of the model and thus often need less training data.

And RFM try to bring that same idea to robotics. You have one big model with general capabilities that can be adapted to a wide range of tasks and robot embodiments.
The general idea from a data perspective is that you train your RFM on many different datasets of different tasks and robots to allow it to build those general capabilities like understanding the physical world around us.
Interestingly, when researchers first started exploring the idea of RFMs, they found that by combining multiple data sources, the model would improve in the individual tasks and often outperform the specialized models, probably thanks to the more general physical understanding.

Und das RFM, das wir gerne verwenden is NVIDIA Isaac GR00T.

Gr00t for Gaming?

Because if you think about it, a video game is basically just a robot embodiment with a screen image as input and controller actions as output.
We provide it with some context about the video game in the prompt and the robot state is just the last controller input.
This is exactly how we are going to let gr00t play video games, so we only need to capture the game screen and the controller input.
